A group is a set equipped with an associative binary operation, an identity element, and an inverse for every element. The operation maps pairs of elements back into the set.

An abelian group is a group whose operation is commutative: for all elements a and b, a combined with b equals b combined with a.

A sequence is an indexed family of elements. A sequence of real numbers can be written as a function from the positive integers to the real numbers.

A Cayley table records a finite binary operation: the entry in row a and column b is the result of applying the operation to a and b in that order.
